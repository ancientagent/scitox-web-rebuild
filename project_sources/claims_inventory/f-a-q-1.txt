Avoid products that coat the hair (e.g., silicones, heavy conditioners ) until after your test, as they may affect test penetration.

How does a hair drug test work?

Labs take a ~1.5-inch hair sample near the scalp (~90 days of history). They test for drug metabolites embedded within the hair shaft , not just surface residues.

Important: A “90-day test” label does not guarantee you’ll pass if you stop for 90 days. With any consistent usage, people can fail for several months and sometimes up to a year+ with head hair. Body hair can reflect 10–13 months .

Why do employers or courts use hair testing?

Hair testing is harder to cheat, detects a longer usage window , and provides a more complete picture than urine or saliva tests.

Can bleaching or dyeing help you pass a test?

Cosmetic changes alone rarely work—labs can still detect metabolites within the hair. SciTOX combines cosmetic changes with targeted chemical treatments designed to remove metabolites.

How soon after use will drugs appear in hair?

Usually about 7 days after use. Around 10–14 days , hair has grown out far enough to be collected. Very recent use might not appear, but the next ~90 days of growth can retain a record.

Can body hair be tested?

Yes—body hair can reflect drug use over an even longer period because it grows more slowly. Typical detection windows are 10–13 months .

Are home hair drug tests reliable?

Some are accurate for screening, but most employers and courts require lab testing with confirmatory methods like GC/MS for official results.

How long does it take (full protocol pacing)?

Plenty of people have used the system in a few hours the day of the test and passed for many substances and usage patterns. However, we recommend pacing over 3–4 days (or even a week if you have time) to minimize hair/scalp strain.

Frequent THC use with little/no clean time: use twice the standard protocol —at least 7–8 rounds of steps 1-2-3-6-7, two rounds of steps 4-5, plus a few bonus rounds, spaced over 6–7 days .

Is it permanent?

It’s permanent on the hair it’s used on and generally permanent for people who have used only a few times—except some edge cases (e.g., certain stimulants) that can grow out sporadically for up to a year, sometimes causing fluctuating results. Any consistent usage is likely to grow out for at least a few months, commonly 6–7 months , and sometimes longer.

If I'm clean for 90 days will I pass?

Not necessarily. The “90-day” label reflects the sample length (~1.5 inches ≈ ~3 months), not a guarantee. Toxins used before those 90 days can still appear. Even a few uses of cocaine or meth can sometimes be detected for a year or more.

If I used once will I pass?

For THC , a single use will often pass, as it usually takes multiple uses for THC-COOH to be detectable. However, a single use of meth, cocaine, some opiates, and most other drugs can still be detected.
