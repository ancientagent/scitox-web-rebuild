Websites + Marketing Help

Track website traffic in Websites + Marketing

Website analytics provide valuable insights into your online presence and business performance. Use these insights to guide your decisions, and improve and grow your business.

  1. Go to your GoDaddy product page.
  2. Scroll down, expand Websites + Marketing and select Manage next to your site.
  3. On your dashboard, under Here’s how you’re performing, you'll find information about your site's health metrics and business performance. You can view this data for the past 30 days or the last 12 months. The metrics shown will depend on your plan and how you've set up your site, but could include:
    • Site visitors
    • Orders
    • Sales
    • Social views
    • Bookings
  4. Select any metric to dive deeper into how each has changed over time.
  5. There's also a cumulative Performance Score that compares your online performance with that of similar businesses.
