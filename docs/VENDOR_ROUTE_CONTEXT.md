## Core Context

This is for the SciTOX website rebuild: https://scitoxdetox.com/

Primary goal: make the existing site vendor-ready for wholesale, smoke shop, head shop, distributor, online retailer, and vendor outreach.

Secondary goal: improve the public customer site so it feels private, serious, warm, direct, competent, and safe for users who may be stressed and looking for guidance.

Strategic direction: the current site needs to be redesigned before vendor outreach because vendors, payment reviewers, and partners will judge the brand by the public site.

## Rebuild Layers

1. Public customer site: private guidance, product routing, calm serious support, trust, privacy, clear policies.
2. Vendor/wholesale layer: product line, product data, inquiry form, wholesale readiness, documentation checklist, restrained claims.

## Constraints

- Do not write final website copy yet.
- Do not approve claims.
- Do not invent proof, testing, reviews, sales numbers, traffic, margins, vendor acceptance, Amazon eligibility, or legal conclusions.
- Mark claim-sensitive content as `[REVIEW REQUIRED]`.
- Mark missing product/business data as `[OWNER DATA NEEDED]`.
- Keep Amazon conditional.
- Treat vendor readiness as the primary goal.
- Remove or review aggressive drug-test language, success-rate claims, guarantee claims, competitor attacks, AI-generated proof, unrelated FAQ/future-product claims, and privacy/security claims that cannot be verified.
- Visual direction: clean, strong, confident, scientific, black/green/white, not spa-like, not panic-sales-page, not headshop gimmick.
