## Project Core

SciTOX is a hair detox product business at `https://scitoxdetox.com/`. The owner says the product works and the business already makes strong money, but the owner personally runs too much of the business, which is the main bottleneck.

The project is a staged audit, rebuild, and expansion-prep effort. The work should stay cautious because the product category can trigger payment processor, ad platform, marketplace, claims, privacy, and vendor-readiness risks.

Do not invent sales, traffic, margins, conversion rates, Amazon eligibility, vendor acceptance, manufacturing capacity, legal conclusions, substantiation, or owner-provided facts.

## Strategic Direction

The plan developed in the shared conversation is not "launch Amazon first." Amazon stays conditional unless research and owner data support it.

The stronger immediate direction is:

1. Clean up the public site and claims risk.
2. Make the site credible enough for partner/vendor review.
3. Build analytics and data visibility before marketing decisions.
4. Reduce owner bottlenecks through support, call, order-question, and knowledge-base automation.
5. Research channels before choosing the first expansion path.
6. Prepare vendor, wholesale, direct-to-consumer, and Amazon paths as conditional options.

## Staged Plan

Stage 1: Owner intake and risk map
- Confirm owner facts before remodel.
- Review the current site, claims, payment risk, marketplace risk, operations, analytics, and red flags.
- Produce templates and task packets for current site snapshot, claims inventory, risk matrix, operations map, and Go/No-Go decision.

Stage 2: Claims and compliance cleanup
- Inventory claims.
- Separate high-risk, substantiated, unsupported, and review-required language.
- Create safer positioning rules before writing final site copy.

Stage 3: Website remodel and credibility strategy
- Redesign structure, trust signals, product pages, FAQ/support, testimonials/results handling, policy/trust areas, mobile-first flow, and vendor-facing credibility.
- Do not write final claims until Stage 2 constraints are respected.

Stage 4: Analytics and data visibility
- Establish GA4/Search Console/tagging/traffic/source/order visibility.
- Treat analytics as a prerequisite for serious marketing decisions.

Stage 5: Operations and support automation
- Map support, calls, customer service, order questions, product instructions, knowledge base, and owner handoff points.
- Use controlled automation that avoids risky claims and routes sensitive cases to humans.

Stage 6: Channel viability research
- Evaluate Amazon, third-party vendors, wholesale/headshops/distributors, affiliate/referral, direct-to-consumer, and paid ads.
- Score channels for fit, restrictions, readiness, operational burden, and blockers.
- Do not contact vendors or select final channels yet.

Stage 7: Expansion roadmap and projections
- Build model structure and scenario logic with placeholders and formulas.
- Treat Amazon as conditional.
- Treat non-Amazon growth as a serious path.
- Prepare final owner-pitch inputs without creating unsupported numbers.

Final pitch framework:
- Explain why website cleanup, operations automation, analytics, and risk cleanup come before aggressive growth.
- Frame Amazon conditionally.
- Present non-Amazon growth as a real path.
- Keep the owner-facing pitch practical for a busy owner who is the bottleneck.

## Avenue Language

The later conversation introduced two related but distinct surfaces:

Avenue 1: Vendor / wholesale readiness
- Goal: make SciTOX credible and lower-risk for vendors, distributors, wholesale buyers, and partner review.
- The public site still matters because vendors will inspect it.
- Site redesign becomes a first step for Avenue 1 because a risky or cluttered public site can deter partners.

Avenue 2: Direct customer site
- Goal: build a warm, serious, private, direct customer experience for stressed customers who need clear guidance fast.
- Do not use "party," "detox miracle," "beat the system," "guaranteed pass," "medical-grade," or unsupported success-rate language.
- Preferred direction: private guidance, clear steps, no judgment, claim-sensitive language marked review-required.

Recommended split:
- Public customer site: private, direct, supportive, claim-restrained.
- Partner/vendor page or deck: more neutral, product-data driven, vendor-ready, and less emotionally charged.

## AI / Intake Guidance

Do not call the intake feature "encrypted AI consultations" unless the actual system supports and has verified that promise.

Safer labels:
- Private Product Guidance
- Confidential Product Intake

The assistant should be controlled, not free-form. It should ask structured questions, avoid legal or medical advice, avoid high-risk claims, route sensitive cases to human support, minimize stored personal data, and provide only approved product guidance.
